The first row is the starting material (SM). Each numbered row after it is one reaction step and describes the product of that step: enter that product's SMILES and MW, not the SM's. The yield is the yield of that step. Paste a SMILES to draw the structure and fill in the MW, or type the MW directly.

Download as Excel, or as an image for slides.

Each step's yield is applied to the moles of the main-line intermediate, and mass is moles × molecular weight. For a convergent step, enter the yield of the coupling step; the building block mass is equivalents × moles of the preceding intermediate.
